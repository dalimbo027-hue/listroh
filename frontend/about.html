About Listroh

Listroh is a community driven platform for discovering, creating, and sharing curated Top 10 lists. Whether you're looking for the best sci-fi films of the decade, essential albums for a road trip, or hidden-gem travel destinations, Listroh helps you find structured, ranked recommendations made by real people.

What we do

The internet is full of scattered opinions, but good recommendations are hard to find in one place. Listroh solves this by organising knowledge into clean, ranked Top 10 lists across the topics people care about most entertainment, music, sports, technology, food, travel, books, gaming, fashion, lifestyle, cars, and science. Every published list is reviewed before it goes live, so what you find is curated rather than automated noise.

How it works

  • Search for any topic and instantly see structured, ranked results.
  • Explore lists published by our community across a dozen categories.
  • Create your own Top 10 lists through the Listroh mobile app and submit them for review.
  • Discover new favourites through lists tagged by category and theme.

Our review process

Every list submitted to Listroh goes through a human moderation step before it is published. Our team checks that lists are genuine, well organised, appropriately tagged, and free of spam or harmful content. Lists that don't meet our quality bar are sent back with feedback so creators can improve them. This is what keeps the Explore page trustworthy and useful.

Who it's for

Listroh is built for the curious people who love finding the best of something and sharing it with others. Casual browsers can explore without an account, while creators can build and publish their own ranked lists to share with the community.

Our commitment

We care about giving people a clean, honest, ad-light experience. Listroh will always prioritise genuine recommendations and user trust over clutter. We follow strict content standards, including a firm commitment to child safety and the removal of any harmful or exploitative material.
